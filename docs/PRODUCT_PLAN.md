# Personal Growth OS — Product Plan

## Product Vision
A local-first personal growth & learning management application connecting long-term goals, daily habits, routines, mindset reflection, and progress reviews.

## Guiding Principles
1. **Local-First & Offline**: User data stays stored locally via IndexedDB.
2. **Action-Oriented**: Bridges high-level life goals with daily practical routines.
3. **No-Cost MVP**: Zero mandatory backend, paid APIs, or external cloud services for V1.0.
